# DAY 6 CURRICULUM — ACID/BASE EQUILIBRIUM DIRECTION

## 1. Purpose and scope
qualitative proton-transfer equilibrium direction using relative pKa. This is readiness preparation, not a replacement for Organic Chemistry I.

### Chemistry accuracy anchors
- Proton-transfer equilibria favor the side with the weaker acid and weaker base.
- The favored side contains the higher-pKa acid.
- Equilibrium preference does not state how fast equilibrium is reached.

## 2. Explicitly out of scope
full equilibrium-constant mathematics and reaction-rate prediction.

## 3. Prior-day handoff and repair
The gate reads Day 4 pKa direction and Day 5 conjugate-base stability. Missing evidence opens a short prerequisite retrieval. A miss routes only that subskill to Review/Needs review and never resets earlier days.

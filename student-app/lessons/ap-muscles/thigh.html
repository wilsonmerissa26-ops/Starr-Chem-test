Locations and actions follow OpenStax Anatomy and Physiology 2e, section 11.6, Appendicular Muscles of the Pelvic Girdle and Lower Limbs, in standard terms (flexion, extension, abduction, adduction, rotation). One OpenStax figure caption disagrees with its own text: it says the pectineus "opens the thigh", but the text says it adducts and flexes the thigh, so the text is used. OpenStax also says the gluteus minimus "brings the thighs back together"; standard anatomy (Kenhub) says it abducts and medially rotates the thigh, and that is used. The vastus intermedius does not show on these figures, so it has a study card and a function question but no pin; piriformis and gluteus minimus details follow OpenStax 11.6 and Kenhub. OpenStax spells it "tensor fascia latae"; the standard name is tensor fasciae latae, and both are accepted. Confirm spelling and required detail against Amerman 3rd ed. and your lab handout.

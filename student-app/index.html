Install on her iPad

On the iPad, open this learning app in Safari. Tap the Share icon, choose Add to Home Screen, keep the name AStarryia Learn, then tap Add. Open that Home Screen icon for future study sessions.

This is a one-device study pilot. After installing, mark a test task complete, close and reopen the app, and confirm it stays checked before relying on its progress tracking.

Keep her progress safe

This pilot saves checkoffs on this iPad/browser. It does not automatically synchronize to another device, and the real iPad still needs a close/reopen durability test.

Full learning backup

Saves everything this app and her chemistry lessons store on this device: planner, Chemistry Coach quiz history, original nine-day coach, whiteboard notes, foundation lesson progress and study songs. Exporting changes nothing. Restoring only adds missing items unless you choose to replace, and replaced versions are kept in a safety copy.

Important: adding the app to the Home Screen may create separate storage from Safari. Export from the version containing her work before switching devices or reinstalling. The export contains task titles and study activity—keep it private.

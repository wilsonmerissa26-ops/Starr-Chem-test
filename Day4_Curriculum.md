# DAY 4 CURRICULUM — ACID/BASE LANGUAGE, CONJUGATES, KA AND PKA

## 1. Purpose and scope
Brønsted acids and bases, conjugate pairs, Ka/pKa direction, acid and conjugate-base strength. This is readiness preparation, not a replacement for Organic Chemistry I.

### Chemistry accuracy anchors
- A Brønsted acid donates H+; a Brønsted base accepts H+.
- Conjugate partners differ by exactly one proton and their charges differ by one.
- pKa = -log Ka; lower pKa means stronger acid and weaker conjugate base.

## 2. Explicitly out of scope

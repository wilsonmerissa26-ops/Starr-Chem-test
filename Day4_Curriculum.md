# DAY 4 CURRICULUM — ACID/BASE LANGUAGE, CONJUGATES, KA AND PKA

## 1. Purpose and scope
Brønsted acids and bases, conjugate pairs, Ka/pKa direction, acid and conjugate-base strength. This is readiness preparation, not a replacement for Organic Chemistry I.

### Chemistry accuracy anchors
- A Brønsted acid donates H+; a Brønsted base accepts H+.
- Conjugate partners differ by exactly one proton and their charges differ by one.
- pKa = -log Ka; lower pKa means stronger acid and weaker conjugate base.

## 2. Explicitly out of scope

## 14. Auditable runtime question and answer-key bank

The wording below is the learner-visible production task. Fallbacks are fresh equivalents and never repair the contaminated original.

### D4-G-DONOR — guided

**Question:** In NH4+ + H2O ⇌ NH3 + H3O+, identify the Brønsted acid and base, and say what each does.

**Chemistry answer key:** NH4+ is the acid because it donates H+; H2O is the base because it accepts H+.

**Semantic evidence required:** `nh4+` or `ammonium`; `acid`; `donat`; `h2o` or `water`; `base`; `accept`.

### D4-I-CONJUGATE — independent

**Question:** Remove H+ from H2PO4−. Give the conjugate base, its charge, and explain the change.

**Chemistry answer key:** HPO4^2− is the conjugate base. Removing H+ lowers the charge by one, from −1 to −2.

**Semantic evidence required:** `hpo4 2-` or `hpo42-` or `hpo4-2`; `conjugate base`; `remove` or `los` or `donat`; `charge` or `-2` or `2-`.

### D4-F-CONJUGATE — independent fallback

**Question:** Fresh equivalent: add H+ to CO3^2−. Give the conjugate acid and explain its charge.

**Chemistry answer key:** HCO3− is the conjugate acid; adding H+ changes −2 to −1.

**Semantic evidence required:** `hco3-` or `bicarbonate`; `conjugate acid`; `add` or `accept`; `-1` or `one less negative`.

### D4-I-PKA — independent

**Question:** An acid has Ka = 1 × 10^-5. Calculate pKa and interpret what a smaller Ka would do to pKa and acid strength.

**Chemistry answer key:** pKa = 5. A smaller Ka gives a higher pKa and a weaker acid.

**Semantic evidence required:** `pka`; `5`; `smaller ka`; `higher` or `increase`; `weaker acid` or `less strong`.

### D4-F-PKA — independent fallback

**Question:** Fresh equivalent: Ka = 1 × 10^-3. Give pKa and compare its strength with an acid of pKa 6.

**Chemistry answer key:** pKa is 3; it is stronger than the pKa 6 acid because lower pKa means stronger acid.

**Semantic evidence required:** `pka`; `3`; `stronger`; `lower pka`.

### D4-I-ACID-STRENGTH — independent

**Question:** Which is the stronger acid: acetic acid (pKa 4.8) or ethanol (pKa 16)? Explain using pKa.

**Chemistry answer key:** Acetic acid is stronger because its pKa, 4.8, is lower than 16.

**Semantic evidence required:** `acetic`; `stronger`; `4.8` or `lower pka`.

### D4-F-ACID — independent fallback

**Question:** Fresh equivalent: compare HCN (pKa 9.2) and NH3 (pKa 38) as acids.

**Chemistry answer key:** HCN is stronger because 9.2 is the lower pKa.

**Semantic evidence required:** `hcn`; `stronger`; `lower pka` or `9.2`.

### D4-I-BASE-STRENGTH — independent

**Question:** Which is the stronger conjugate base: acetate (conjugate acid pKa 4.8) or ethoxide (conjugate acid pKa 16)? Explain.

**Chemistry answer key:** Ethoxide is stronger: its conjugate acid has the higher pKa and is the weaker acid.

**Semantic evidence required:** `ethoxide` or `ch3ch2o-`; `stronger base`; `higher pka` or `16`; `weaker acid`.

### D4-F-BASE — independent fallback

**Question:** Fresh equivalent: compare CN− (HCN pKa 9.2) with Cl− (HCl pKa −7) as bases.

**Chemistry answer key:** CN− is stronger because its conjugate acid has the higher pKa.

**Semantic evidence required:** `cn-` or `cyanide`; `stronger`; `higher pka` or `9.2`.

### D4-T-PYRIDINIUM — transfer

**Question:** Pyridinium has pKa 5.2 and anilinium pKa 4.6. Name the stronger acid and the stronger conjugate base, explaining both directions.

**Chemistry answer key:** Anilinium is the stronger acid (lower pKa); pyridine is the stronger conjugate base because pyridinium is the weaker, higher-pKa acid.

**Semantic evidence required:** `anilinium`; `stronger acid`; `lower pka`; `pyridine`; `stronger` or `base`; `higher pka`.

### D4-T-PYRIDINIUM-FALLBACK — transfer fallback

**Question:** Fresh transfer: HF has pKa 3.2 and HCN 9.2. Identify the stronger acid and stronger conjugate base.

**Chemistry answer key:** HF is the stronger acid; CN− is the stronger base because HCN has the higher pKa.

**Semantic evidence required:** `hf`; `stronger acid`; `lower pka`; `cn-` or `cyanide`; `stronger base`; `higher pka`.

## 15. Support, retrieval, and routing behavior

The cold prompt shows no definition, choices, word bank, or support visual. Hint identifies the feature to inspect; First step asks for the species/result before its reason; Walkthrough and IDK reveal the complete keyed reasoning and contaminate that encounter. Wrong answers remain on the same problem for bounded repair. A correct contaminated response routes to the named fresh equivalent. Only a first-attempt, unsupported response to an Independent item counts. After supported teach-back, the later fallback is a fresh no-clue retrieval with a reset attempt budget. Notebook records clean versus supported encounters; Review records the misconception code; Summary reports clean evidence and transfer. Missing prerequisite evidence routes to that day’s Review without resetting completed days.

# Vocabulary Support Escalation Policy

This policy applies to production-first vocabulary learning on Day 1, Day 3, and future lessons that reuse the teaching framework.

## Core rule

The learner is never required to keep guessing a vocabulary concept indefinitely.

A **written attempt** means the learner submits an explanation and/or application response for grading. An explicit **I do not know yet** request is a request for teaching, not a failed written explanation and does not consume one of the three attempts.

The three-attempt ceiling is **per encounter**, not a lifetime counter. Direct teaching ends the current attempt streak. When the term returns later for no-clue retrieval, that later encounter starts with a fresh attempt budget while the earlier learning history remains preserved.

### Escalation sequence

1. **First unsuccessful written attempt:** keep the learner's answer, identify what was understood and what is missing or conflicting, and provide targeted repair support. A word bank may appear only after this miss.
2. **Second unsuccessful written attempt:** continue targeted repair and make the alternate visual/animated representation available. Do not mark the term Independent.
3. **Third unsuccessful written attempt:** stop testing and teach. Show the complete definition in plain language and the correct answer/reasoning for the current application question. Do not require a fourth guess before providing the answer.
4. **I do not know yet:** teaching begins immediately without recording a failed written explanation.

## After direct teaching

Direct teaching is support, not mastery evidence.

- The complete definition, application answer, word bank, and visual are hidden before the learner explains the term back.
- Immediate hide-and-recall after teaching is **Supported**, even when correct.
- A supported term must return later for a fresh no-clue written definition plus application before it can become **Independent**.
- The later no-clue encounter receives a fresh three-attempt budget; pre-teaching misses do not make the system re-teach early.
- If the learner still cannot explain the concept after direct teaching and a bounded repair opportunity, mark it **Needs review** and move on. If the learner selects **I do not know yet** during immediate post-teaching recall, also mark **Needs review** and move on rather than showing the answer repeatedly.

## Visual rule

Visuals and animations are teaching representations. They may appear during repair or direct teaching but must not remain visible during cold or later no-clue retrieval.

## Evidence rule

Recognition, matching, word-bank use, visual identification, immediate teach-back, copying the supplied answer, a teaching-boundary marker, or requesting **I do not know yet** cannot independently establish vocabulary mastery.

Independent vocabulary evidence requires a correct no-clue explanation and application after support has been removed.

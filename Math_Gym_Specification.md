# MATH GYM — SPECIFICATION
## Companion to the Day 1 Curriculum. Infrastructure, not a one-day feature.

**Governing rule: generate the answer first, build the problem around it.** Chemistry uses a curated individually verified bank, never procedural molecule generation.

# PART 1: MATH GENERATION ALGORITHMS

Fractions use exact integer arithmetic and reduce answers. “X of Y” chooses divisible wholes. Percent problems are answer-first and produce clean results.

Algebra chooses x first, chooses coefficients, and computes the remaining constant so ax+b=cx+d has the chosen clean solution. Formula rearrangement uses a curated set.

Exponents choose a base and exponent, computing reciprocals exactly for negative exponents. Product and quotient items apply same-base rules.

Scientific notation combines front numbers and exponents, then renormalizes the coefficient into [1,10).

Logs choose a true power or front digit and exponent; estimation keys use the actual logarithm and accept ±0.15.

Unit conversions use a verified factor table. Stacked rates chain amount and time conversions and require the final unit label.

# DAY 5 CURRICULUM — WHY ONE ACID IS STRONGER: CONJUGATE-BASE STABILITY

## 1. Purpose and scope
structure-based acidity comparisons using atom, resonance, induction, charge location, and readiness-level hybridization. This is readiness preparation, not a replacement for Organic Chemistry I.

### Chemistry accuracy anchors
- The stronger acid forms the more stable conjugate base.
- Carboxylate charge is delocalized over two oxygens; resonance moves electrons, not atoms.
- Electron-withdrawing groups stabilize nearby negative charge inductively, and the effect weakens with distance.

The high-yield 5-check method

Every reaction starts here.

1. Leaving groupIs it good enough to leave? Br, I, OTs good; untreated OH poor.
2. Carbon typeIs carbon bearing LG methyl, 1°, 2°, or 3°?
3. ReagentStrong nucleophile? Strong base? Bulky base? Weak neutral?
4. Solvent + heatPolar aprotic favors SN2; polar protic often supports ionization.
5. Product constraints: E1/E2 need β-H; E2 needs anti H–C–C–LG; SN2 inverts; carbocations may rearrange.

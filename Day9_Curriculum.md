# DAY 9 CURRICULUM — INTEGRATED READINESS TRANSFER / CAPSTONE

## 1. Purpose and scope
bounded integration of structure, resonance, acid/base, electron flow, and energy reasoning. This is readiness preparation, not a replacement for Organic Chemistry I.

### Chemistry accuracy anchors
- Capstone evidence must be fresh and unsupported.
- A weak subskill routes to its Review target without restarting all days.
- Coverage spans structure/formal charge, stability, acid/base direction, electron flow, and thermodynamics versus kinetics.

## 2. Explicitly out of scope
untaught mechanisms, reaction taxonomy, synthesis, spectroscopy, and a full Organic Chemistry I course.

## 3. Prior-day handoff and repair
The gate reads Days 4-8 evidence, with surgical Review routing for any weak prerequisite. Missing evidence opens a short prerequisite retrieval. A miss routes only that subskill to Review/Needs review and never resets earlier days.

## 4. Teaching examples (contaminated)
Teacher demonstration TCH-A and visual TCH-V expose the complete reasoning. They may create Seen/Guided evidence only and can never appear in the independent or transfer banks.

## 5. Guided examples
G1 names each reasoning step with prompts. G2 removes the final-answer prompt. Any help preserves the response, diagnoses the error, and contaminates that item.

## 6. Fresh independent bank and tags
- `D9-I-STRUCTURE-ACID` — independent, no-clue, fresh, chemistry-production
- `D9-I-EQUILIBRIUM-FLOW` — independent, no-clue, fresh, chemistry-production
- `D9-I-ENERGY` — independent, no-clue, fresh, chemistry-production

## 7. Sufficient-evidence stop rule
Collect clean evidence on 3 distinct required independent tags, then one clean transfer. Stop immediately afterward. A wrong attempt or answer-bearing help makes only that item ineligible; the learner receives a fresh equivalent.

## 8. Transfer and fallback
- `D9-T-INTEGRATED` — different-looking cold transfer.
- `D9-T-INTEGRATED-FALLBACK` — equivalent fallback, used only if the first item is contaminated.

## 9. Error codes and repair routes
- `FORMAL_CHARGE` — preserve the answer, contrast the relevant chemical relationship, then require a fresh item for mastery.
- `RESONANCE` — preserve the answer, contrast the relevant chemical relationship, then require a fresh item for mastery.
- `ACID_BASE` — preserve the answer, contrast the relevant chemical relationship, then require a fresh item for mastery.
- `ELECTRON_FLOW` — preserve the answer, contrast the relevant chemical relationship, then require a fresh item for mastery.
- `ENERGY` — preserve the answer, contrast the relevant chemical relationship, then require a fresh item for mastery.
- `IDK` — directly teach; it is not counted as a failed written explanation.
- `INCOMPLETE` — identify the correct fragment and the missing relationship. On the second repeat, change representation. After three unsuccessful written explanations, teach completely and begin a fresh encounter.

## 10. Vocabulary and visuals
Production-first terms: No large new vocabulary set. The first cold prompt supplies no definition, choices, or word bank. No visual cues in cold proof; prior-day visuals appear only after support is requested. Visual support never earns Independent evidence and respects reduced motion.

## 11. Learner state and support contract
Hint points to one feature; First step identifies the first operation without completing it; Walkthrough teaches the full chain. Guided, helped, or previously missed items remain Supported/Developing. State uses `astarryia.day9.v1`; corrupt or non-v1 data safely starts fresh. Practice is bounded.

## 12. Final status and handoff
Independent items plus clean transfer earn Transfer and unlock Day 9 completion. Otherwise record Needs review with tagged routing; never loop indefinitely. Notebook entries record item, stage, and whether evidence was clean.

## 13. Contract acceptance
Tests must cover syntax, these chemical anchors, state transitions, natural-language variants, adversarial misconceptions, support/mastery separation, storage migration, and mobile/reduced-motion markup.

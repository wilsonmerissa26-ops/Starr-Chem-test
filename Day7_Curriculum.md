# DAY 7 CURRICULUM — NUCLEOPHILE, ELECTROPHILE, POLARITY, AND ELECTRON FLOW

## 1. Purpose and scope
electron-pair donors/acceptors, polarized bonds, and two-arrow substitution readiness. This is readiness preparation, not a replacement for Organic Chemistry I.

### Chemistry accuracy anchors
- A nucleophile donates an electron pair; an electrophile accepts it.
- Neutral CH3Br has an electrophilic carbon because the C-Br bond is polarized.
- Arrow tails begin at electrons; in HO- plus CH3Br, one arrow forms C-O and the C-Br pair goes to Br.

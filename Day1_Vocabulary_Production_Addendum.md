# DAY 1 VOCABULARY PRODUCTION ADDENDUM

This addendum supersedes the recognition-only behavior of `day1/chemistry-vocabulary-v24.js` for the six Day 1 chemistry terms. The old v24 file remains historical only and is not loaded by the live Day 1 page.

## Required evidence

For each term, the learner must first see only the term and then:

1. write what the term means in her own words with no definition, word bank, or answer choices visible; and
2. answer a short application question that requires using the idea.

The live grader checks required idea-parts and conflicting ideas. It must not award success to a pile of familiar clue words that does not form an explanation.

## Support

After an incomplete or incorrect answer, the app may identify what the learner already explained, what is missing, and what conflicts with the concept. Only then may a word bank appear. The learner must revise her own explanation; the app must not assemble the answer for her.

If explicit teaching is requested, the definition may be shown once and then hidden. The immediate hide-and-recall attempt remains **Supported** even when correct.

## Mastery state

Only a correct no-clue written explanation plus application can mark a term **Independent**. A term completed with a word bank, explicit teaching, or another clue must return later for a fresh no-clue retrieval before it can become Independent.

Recognition games such as matching or multiple choice are practice only and cannot clear a term.

The six vocabulary terms remain separate from chemistry mastery evidence. This vocabulary layer does not call the Day 1 mastery orchestrator or write chemistry mastery evidence.

## Six terms

- Valence electron
- Bond
- Lone pair
- Lewis structure
- Octet
- Central atom

# DAY 1 — INTERACTIVE LAYER SPECIFICATION
## Companion to Day1_Curriculum.md and Math_Gym_Specification.md

# CHANGE 1: SHOW ME MODE AND THE WORKBENCH ARE THE SAME COMPONENT

They are one component: the Molecule Stage, with Watch and Build modes. Watch is Dr. Merissa-driven and advances only on learner taps. Build is learner-driven with the same visual language.

# CHANGE 2: TWO REPRESENTATIONAL VIEWS FOR DAY 1, NOT THREE

Day 1 ships Symbolic and Particulate views. Geometry is not taught today.

# CHANGE 3: THE ELECTRON BUILDER MERGES INTO THE WATCH-MODE SEQUENCE

An atom with six dots appears; the learner identifies oxygen; two hydrogens appear; the learner places them; shared pairs animate into bonds; Symbolic and Particulate views appear side by side.

# THE WORKBENCH

Tap an atom from C, N, O, H, then tap the stage. Tap two atoms to bond. Select Lone Pair, then tap an atom. Undo reverses one action. Reset confirms before clearing. Dragging is optional, never required. Bond-order controls are not exposed on Day 1.

| Phase | Counter |
|---|---|
| Watch | Always visible |
| Independent | Hidden until Hint 1 or beyond |
| Fresh mastery | Never present; no reveal control |

# BUILD MODE: TAP-FIRST

All touch targets are comfortable for a phone. No response requires dragging. Core interactions are owned locally and do not depend on external simulations.
